Ledger Link sends your World of Warcraft: Forever character, guild roster and raid boss pulls to Raid Ledger, so your roster, gear and raid history stay current without retyping anything.

Addons can't connect to the internet, so Ledger Link works like WeakAuras or Details! exports. It builds the data, compresses it into a string, and shows it in a box. You copy the string and paste it into Raid Ledger.

How to use it

  1. Click the Ledger Link minimap button (or type /rl) to open the panel.
  2. Pick your realm's ruleset once on each character: Normal, PvP, RP or Hardcore.
  3. Click Export all (one paste). A box opens with your character, guild and raid strings.
  4. Click the text to select all of it, then press Ctrl+C.
  5. In Raid Ledger, open your character, choose Import string, and paste.

You can also export each part on its own with the Character, Guild and Raid buttons.

What it exports

  • Character: your gear, talents, and raid and dungeon lockouts.
  • Guild: your guild roster, up to 2,000 members, with names, ranks, levels, classes and when each member was last online.
  • Raid: your last 50 boss pulls. While the addon is loaded, it records each pull by itself: the encounter, difficulty, kill or wipe, start and end times, and who was in your group. It doesn't record damage or DPS, because WoW: Forever doesn't let addons read the combat log.

A guild with more than 250 members exports in pages. Export all puts every page in the same box. If you export the guild on its own, use Next > to step through the pages, copy each one, and paste them all into the same import box, in any order.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/rlOpens the Ledger Link panel
/rl export allCharacter, guild and raid in one box, pasted into Raid Ledger together
/rl export char | guild | raidExports one part on its own
/rl ruleset normal | pvp | rp | hardcoreSets this character's realm ruleset
/rl guildnotes on | offAdds public guild notes to the guild export (off by default)
/rl raid / /rl raid clearShows or clears your recorded boss pulls
/rl region us | eu | kr | twYour region, only needed if the game doesn't report it (the panel shows these buttons when that happens)
/rl minimap on | offShows or hides the minimap button
/rl statusShows your last export times
/rl probeA diagnostic report to include in bug reports
/rl helpLists every command

/ledgerlink works the same as /rl, in case another addon already uses /rl.

Privacy

  • Officer notes are never read or exported, whatever your guild rank.
  • Public guild notes are only included if you turn them on.
  • Nothing leaves your game unless you copy a string and paste it somewhere yourself.
